# Scheme: Bihar SC/ST Civil Services Coaching Scheme (BPSC Free Coaching)

## Category
Education / Competitive Exam Support / Bihar State Scheme

## State Coverage
Bihar (State Government, via Bihar SC/ST Welfare Department)

## Benefit
Free coaching for BPSC (Bihar Public Service Commission) exams and UPSC Civil Services for SC/ST students:
- Free coaching at designated coaching institutes
- Stipend of ₹1,500–₹3,000/month during coaching period
- Study material and books provided
- Accommodation support in some schemes

## Eligibility criteria (plain language)
- Must belong to Scheduled Caste or Scheduled Tribe (proof required)
- Must be a resident of Bihar
- Age: as per BPSC/UPSC eligibility (21–37 years generally, with relaxations for SC/ST)
- Educational qualification: graduation from a recognized university
- Family income: below ₹8 lakh per annum (in most iterations of the scheme)
- Must not have availed similar coaching benefit previously from state government

## Required documents (ranked by how often applicants miss them)
1. Caste certificate (SC/ST) issued by competent authority in Bihar — must be current, not expired
2. Aadhaar card
3. Bihar domicile/residential certificate
4. Graduation certificate and marksheet
5. Income certificate from competent authority (BDO/SDO/Tehsildar) — must show family income
6. Bank account in applicant's name linked to Aadhaar (for stipend transfer)
7. Declaration of not having availed similar scheme before
8. Passport-size photograph

## Common rejection reasons
- Caste certificate not issued by Bihar authority (certificates from other states not accepted)
- Income certificate expired or showing income above threshold
- Graduation degree from unrecognized university
- Applicant already received similar coaching support from state
- Age above the prescribed limit

## Common silent/ghosted causes (labeled as inference)
- Application submitted online but state portal having backlog in processing
- Seat allocation to coaching institute completed before application was processed
- Stipend DBT failure due to bank account not Aadhaar-linked

## How to check status
- state.bihar.gov.in → Welfare Department → SC/ST Welfare
- Helpline: Bihar SC/ST Welfare Department, Patna
