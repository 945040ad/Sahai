# Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY) — LPG Connection

## Category
Energy / Clean Cooking Fuel / Women Welfare

## State Coverage
Pan-India (Central Government Scheme)

## Benefit
- Free LPG connection (security deposit waived by government)
- First refill and hotplate provided free (Ujjwala 2.0 onwards)
- Subsidy on LPG cylinders via DBT (Direct Benefit Transfer) to Aadhaar-linked bank account
- No cost to the Below-Poverty-Line household for the initial connection

## Eligibility criteria (plain language)
- Adult woman (18+) from a Below Poverty Line household
- Must not already have an LPG connection in the household at the same address
- Must have Aadhaar card and a bank account
- Eligibility categories (Ujjwala 2.0 — expanded):
  - SECC 2011 listed households
  - SC/ST households
  - Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Gramin) beneficiaries
  - Antyodaya Anna Yojana cardholders
  - Forest dwellers
  - Tea and ex-tea garden tribes (Assam)
  - People living in islands/river islands
  - Migrants (Ujjwala 2.0 specific: self-declaration of address accepted — no domicile proof needed)

## Required documents (ranked by how often applicants miss them)
1. Aadhaar card (identity + KYC for the woman applicant)
2. Ration card — BPL/Antyodaya — showing household members; or any of the expanded eligibility category proof
3. Bank account in the woman's own name linked to Aadhaar (for DBT subsidy)
4. Self-declaration that no LPG connection exists at the address (critical — if any family member has a connection at the same address, application is rejected)
5. Address proof — Ujjwala 2.0 allows self-declaration for migrants without fixed address proof

## Common rejection reasons
- Another LPG connection already exists at the same address (even in a family member's or landlord's name in some cases)
- Bank account not in woman applicant's name (must be her own account, not husband's or joint)
- Aadhaar name mismatch with bank account or ration card
- Household income shown above BPL threshold during distributor verification
- Migrant applicants not knowing they can submit self-declaration (rejected for not having domicile proof)

## Common silent/ghosted causes (labeled as inference, not confirmed)
- Distributor (LPG agency) not processing applications despite submission — probable in areas with new distributor backlogs
- KYC verification failure at oil company (HPCL/BPCL/IOC) backend — probable if Aadhaar eKYC fails
- Application submitted at wrong distributor (must be a PMUY-empanelled outlet in your area)

## How to check status
- mylpg.in → Ujjwala status check
- pmuy.gov.in
- Call your LPG distributor directly with the application number
- Helpline: 1906 (LPG emergency + enquiry)
