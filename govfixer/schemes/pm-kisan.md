# Scheme: PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi

## Category
Agriculture / Income Support

## State Coverage
Pan-India (Central Government Scheme)

## Benefit
₹6,000/year in 3 equal installments of ₹2,000 each, directly to bank account

## Eligibility criteria (plain language)
- Must be a landholding farmer family (husband, wife, minor children)
- Must have cultivable land in their name as per land records
- Excludes: institutional landholders (trusts, companies)
- Excludes: current or former government employees (Central/State) drawing ≥₹10,000/month pension, except Class IV/Group D
- Excludes: income-tax payers in the last assessment year
- Excludes: professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, CAs) and their family members
- Excludes: retired/former MPs, MLAs, Ministers, Mayors, District Panchayat Chairpersons
- Small/marginal farmers with less than 2 hectares are the primary target, but all landholding farmers are now eligible

## Required documents (ranked by how often applicants miss them)
1. Aadhaar card — #1 rejection cause; name must exactly match land records and bank account
2. Land ownership records (Khatoni/Khata) — must be updated in state revenue records
3. Bank account linked to Aadhaar (DBT-enabled) — IFSC code must be correct
4. Mobile number linked to Aadhaar (for OTP verification)
5. Self-declaration form for exclusion criteria (not being an income-tax payer, not a govt. employee, etc.)

## Common rejection reasons
- Aadhaar-bank account name mismatch (most common — even spelling differences cause rejection)
- Land record not updated in state revenue records (e.g., inherited land not mutated in Khatoni)
- Duplicate farmer entry (same Aadhaar registered in two states or by two family members)
- Aadhaar not seeded with bank account
- Wrong IFSC code entered during registration
- Beneficiary falls under excluded category (govt. employee, income-tax payer) — even if declared incorrectly

## Common silent/ghosted causes (labeled as inference, not confirmed)
- Application stuck at state-level verification queue (state nodal officer approval pending) — probable if >60 days with no update
- Aadhaar authentication failure at UIDAI — probable if name mismatch exists
- Bank account dormant/frozen — DBT transfer bounces silently
- State revenue department records not synced with PM-Kisan portal — land records exist locally but not digitally updated

## How to check status
- pmkisan.gov.in → Farmers Corner → Beneficiary Status (enter Aadhaar/mobile/account)
- Helpline: 155261 / 011-24300606
