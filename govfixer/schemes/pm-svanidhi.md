# Scheme: PM SVANidhi – PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi

## Category
Urban Livelihood / Microfinance / Street Vendors

## State Coverage
Pan-India (for urban street vendors)

## Benefit
- First loan: ₹10,000 (collateral-free working capital)
- Second loan: ₹20,000 (on timely repayment of first)
- Third loan: ₹50,000 (on timely repayment of second)
- Digital transaction incentive: up to ₹1,200/year cashback for digital payments
- Credit guarantee covered by government (no collateral required)

## Eligibility criteria (plain language)
- Must be a street vendor vending in urban areas as of or before March 24, 2020
- Must have a Certificate of Vending (CoV) OR Letter of Recommendation (LoR) from Urban Local Body (ULB)
- Vendors identified in the survey conducted by ULBs under PM SVANidhi are directly eligible
- Vendors not in ULB survey can apply with LoR from ULB or Town Vending Committee (TVC)
- No minimum education, no minimum income requirement
- Even vendors who were vending on the outskirts of urban areas or peri-urban areas are eligible if the ULB includes them

## Required documents (ranked by how often applicants miss them)
1. Certificate of Vending (CoV) from ULB — or Letter of Recommendation (LoR) if not yet issued a CoV; this is the #1 blocker
2. Aadhaar card
3. Bank account linked to Aadhaar (DBT-enabled); even a Jan Dhan account works
4. Vendor ID card (if issued by ULB)
5. Passport-size photograph
6. Mobile number (for OTP and digital transaction tracking)

## Common rejection reasons
- No CoV or LoR from ULB — vendor not in the official survey list and hasn't obtained LoR
- Bank account not Aadhaar-seeded (DBT failure on first disbursement)
- CIBIL/credit bureau check flagged an existing default (even microfinance MFI loans count)
- Vendor classified as rural, but applied on urban scheme
- Photograph or signature mismatch during bank processing
- Vendor listed as "lender" or "shop owner" by bank instead of street vendor (occupational mismatch)

## Common silent/ghosted causes (labeled as inference, not confirmed)
- Application submitted on pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in but not forwarded by lending institution to bank — probable if >30 days with no movement
- Bank branch unfamiliar with the scheme — common with smaller co-operative banks
- ULB list upload to SVANidhi portal delayed — vendor's name doesn't appear even though they're in ULB records

## How to check status
- pmsvanidhi.mohua.gov.in → Track Application
- Helpline: 1800-11-1979
