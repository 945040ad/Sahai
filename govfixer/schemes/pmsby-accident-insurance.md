# Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) — Accident Insurance

## Category
Insurance / Social Security / Accidental Death & Disability

## State Coverage
Pan-India (through banks and post offices)

## Benefit
- ₹2 lakh for accidental death or total permanent disability
- ₹1 lakh for partial permanent disability
- Annual premium: ₹20/year (auto-debited from bank account in May/June)
- Covers road accidents, drowning, snake bite, electrocution, falling — any accident

## Eligibility criteria (plain language)
- Age: 18 to 70 years
- Must have a savings bank account (any bank or post office)
- Must give consent for auto-debit of ₹20/year premium from bank account
- People with multiple bank accounts can enroll under only ONE account
- Renewal is annual and automatic if auto-debit succeeds

## Required documents (ranked by how often applicants miss them)
1. Bank account (savings) — primary requirement; Aadhaar linkage recommended
2. Consent-cum-Declaration form (auto-debit authorization) — signed and submitted to bank
3. Nominee details — must be registered; this is the #1 cause of claim delays/rejections

## Common rejection reasons (primarily at CLAIM stage)
- Nominee not registered or nominee's identity documents not matching at claim
- Policy lapsed — premium auto-debit failed because of insufficient balance (₹20 is tiny but accounts with zero balance still fail)
- Death/disability caused by suicide, self-inflicted injury, or intoxication — explicitly excluded
- Death classified as "natural" rather than accidental by post-mortem report (common dispute)
- FIR not filed (for accidents — FIR is required for accident claims; if there's no FIR, claim gets stuck)
- Claim not filed within 30 days of accident (late filing = rejection by insurer)
- Multiple policies trying to claim twice (only one policy pays)

## Common silent/ghosted causes (at enrollment, labeled as inference)
- Auto-debit consent not recorded by bank branch — form submitted but not entered in insurer system
- Bank did not forward enrollment to the designated insurance company (PMSBY runs through New India Assurance and other PSU insurers via banks)

## Critical claim requirements (often missed)
- File an FIR immediately for any accident — without FIR, claim WILL be rejected
- Post-mortem report for death claims — hospital must mention cause as accidental
- Claim form must be submitted by nominee to the bank within 30 days
- Disability certificate from a civil surgeon / government doctor for disability claims

## How to check status
- Contact your bank branch (enrollment and claims both processed through bank)
- jansuraksha.gov.in for scheme details and claim forms
- State Insurance helpline varies by insurer
