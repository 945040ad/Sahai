# Scheme: Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (AB-PMJAY)

## Category
Health / Insurance

## State Coverage
Pan-India (Central + State partnership; some states have their own top-up schemes)

## Benefit
Health cover of ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation. Cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals. No cap on family size or age.

## Eligibility criteria (plain language)
- Automatically determined from SECC 2011 database — no application required if you're in the database
- Rural: families in any of these SECC deprivation categories (D1–D7): manual scavenging, primitive tribal groups, legally released bonded labourers, SC/ST households, families with no adult member aged 16–59, female-headed households with no adult male member aged 16–59, households with disabled member and no able-bodied adult, destitute, living on alms
- Urban: 11 occupational categories (rag pickers, domestic workers, street vendors, construction workers, etc.)
- Does NOT cover: government employees with CGHS or state health scheme, income-tax payers
- Note: many states have expanded beyond SECC (e.g., Uttarakhand added all residents below a threshold) — check state-specific rules

## Required documents (ranked by how often applicants miss them)
1. Aadhaar card — identity verification at hospital; must match PMJAY database
2. Ration card (for family member linkage when Aadhaar of dependents is missing)
3. Ayushman card (the plastic/digital card generated after eligibility verification — get this BEFORE hospitalization)
4. Mobile number linked to Aadhaar (for OTP-based verification at hospital)

## Common rejection reasons
- Name/family not in SECC database — the single biggest blocker; no workaround without state-level grievance
- Aadhaar name mismatch with PMJAY database (common with nicknames, different spellings)
- Hospital not empanelled under PMJAY (patient chose a hospital that doesn't accept PMJAY)
- Treatment category not covered (PMJAY covers 1,949+ procedures; dental, OPD, cosmetic are excluded)
- Pre-existing condition claimed to be excluded by hospital (wrong — PMJAY covers ALL pre-existing conditions from Day 1)
- Claim rejected because patient was treated at non-NABH hospital for a procedure that requires it

## Common silent/ghosted causes (labeled as inference, not confirmed)
- Ayushman card not yet generated — eligibility exists but card generation pending at CSC/hospital
- UIDAI eKYC failure — Aadhaar authentication timeout or biometric mismatch at hospital
- Family member's name missing from the household list in PMJAY — can add via grievance portal

## How to check status
- beneficiary.nha.gov.in — check eligibility by mobile/Aadhaar
- PMJAY helpline: 14555
- NHA Grievance Portal: grievance.pmjay.gov.in
