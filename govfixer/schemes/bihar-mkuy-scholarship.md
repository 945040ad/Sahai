# Scheme: Bihar State Scholarship – Mukhyamantri Kanya Utthan Yojana (MKUY)

## Category
Education / Women Empowerment / Scholarship

## State Coverage
Bihar (State Government Scheme)

## Benefit
- Birth: ₹2,000 (on birth of girl child)
- Vaccination: ₹1,000 (on complete vaccination)
- Class 10 pass: ₹10,000 (for girls who pass 10th with 1st division)
- Class 12 pass (unmarried): ₹25,000
- Graduation (unmarried at time of application): ₹50,000

## Eligibility criteria (plain language)
- Only for girl students domiciled in Bihar
- Must be unmarried at the time of graduation disbursement (for the ₹50,000 component)
- Must have passed from a recognized institution/university in Bihar
- One benefit per family per component (only one daughter's graduation incentive paid if multiple daughters graduate in same year — actually, ALL eligible daughters are covered per official rules, verify at portal)
- Class 10/12 incentives require first division (≥60%) for most components
- Graduation incentive available for any stream: BA, BSc, BCom, B.Tech, MBBS, etc. from Bihar recognized institutions

## Required documents (ranked by how often applicants miss them)
1. Aadhaar card (must match registration name exactly)
2. Bank account in girl student's own name (not parent's account) — linked to Aadhaar
3. Graduation/10th/12th marksheet and certificate from Bihar board/university
4. Bihar domicile/residential certificate
5. Unmarried certificate from Block Development Officer or Notarized affidavit
6. Photograph (passport size, white background)
7. Student ID from institution
8. Mobile number registered on portal — OTP-based verification

## Common rejection reasons
- Name mismatch between Aadhaar and marksheet (most common — even one letter difference)
- Bank account in parent's name, not student's own name
- University/college not recognized or not on the portal's approved list
- Institution result not yet uploaded to the state portal (application submitted before university data sync)
- Duplicate application from same Aadhaar
- Residential certificate from outside Bihar
- Unmarried status not certified by competent authority (plain affidavit rejected in some cases)

## Common silent/ghosted causes (labeled as inference, not confirmed)
- Application at pending state at DTE (Directorate of Technical Education) or university verification stage
- University not submitted student list to state portal — student is eligible but university upload pending
- DBT failure because bank account not activated or has zero balance (some banks deactivate dormant accounts)
- Application verified but awaiting fund release in current fiscal quarter
