# Scheme: Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act (MGNREGA)

## Category
Employment / Rural Livelihood / Rights-based

## State Coverage
Pan-India (Rural areas only)

## Benefit
- Guaranteed 100 days of wage employment per household per year (150 days in drought-notified areas)
- Daily wage as per state notification (Bihar: ₹245/day as of 2025)
- Work must be provided within 15 days of demand — if not, unemployment allowance is payable
- Payment within 15 days of work completion
- All work within 5 km of applicant's village

## Eligibility criteria (plain language)
- Must be a rural household (not in urban municipal area)
- Any adult member (18+) who wants to do unskilled manual work
- Must have a job card issued by the Gram Panchayat
- No income bar — this is a right-based scheme, not means-tested
- Any number of household members can work, subject to 100-day household cap

## Required documents (ranked by how often applicants miss them)
1. MGNREGA Job Card (issued by Gram Panchayat) — this is your access document; without it nothing works
2. Aadhaar card (for wage payment via Aadhaar-linked bank/post office account)
3. Bank/Post Office account linked to Aadhaar (for wage credit)
4. Photograph (for job card issuance)

## Common rejection reasons (for wage payment / work denial)
- Job card not issued or lost and not replaced (most common blocking issue)
- Aadhaar not seeded with bank/post office account — wages can't be transferred
- Worker name not entered in muster roll despite working (Mate/Supervisor's failure)
- Bank account not active or frozen (zero balance accounts get deactivated in some banks)
- Work demanded verbally but not formally registered as written demand — GP may deny demand was made

## Common silent/ghosted causes (labeled as inference)
- Work not allocated within 15 days but unemployment allowance not paid (government non-compliance — very common)
- Muster roll not submitted by supervisor, so wages not processed
- FTO (Fund Transfer Order) generated but bank-level failure in credit — NeFMS transfer pending
- Workers' Aadhaar authentication failure at payment stage (NPCI seeding mismatch)

## How to check status
- nreganpis.nic.in → MIS Reports → Job Card, Muster Roll, Payment
- nrega.nic.in → Citizen corner
- Contact Gram Panchayat Rozgar Sevak / Block Programme Officer
- Helpline: 1800-111-555 (MoRD toll-free)
