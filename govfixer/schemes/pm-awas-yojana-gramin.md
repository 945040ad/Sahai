# Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana – Gramin (PMAY-G)

## Category
Housing / Rural

## State Coverage
Pan-India (Central Government Scheme, implemented via States)

## Benefit
₹1.20 lakh (plain areas) or ₹1.30 lakh (hilly/NE states) for house construction. ₹12,000 additional for toilet under SBM-G. MGNREGS wages for 90–95 person-days.

## Eligibility criteria (plain language)
- Must be from a household listed in the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 data — if your name isn't in SECC, you are NOT eligible under the automatic selection process (though Gram Sabha can include you in an awaiting list in some states)
- Must be homeless OR living in a kutcha house (zero or one room with kutcha walls and roof)
- Priority order: Scheduled Castes/Tribes, minorities, freed bonded labourers, widows/kin of defence personnel killed in action, ex-servicemen, persons with disabilities, women-headed households
- Excludes: families owning a pucca house anywhere in India
- Excludes: families with motorized 2/3/4-wheelers, mechanized farm equipment, or a Kisan Credit Card with credit limit ≥₹50,000
- Excludes: families with a government employee
- Excludes: families paying income tax, or with a family member earning ≥₹15,000/month

## Required documents (ranked by how often applicants miss them)
1. SECC 2011 inclusion proof — if your name is missing, this blocks everything else
2. Aadhaar card (all adult household members)
3. Bank account linked to Aadhaar (DBT-enabled) — money is released in instalments
4. Job card (MGNREGS) — for claiming labour component wages
5. Land ownership/No Objection Certificate — proof you have land to build on (doesn't need to own land in some states, NOC from village body accepted)
6. Photograph of existing dwelling (kutcha house) — often asked during verification
7. Caste certificate (for SC/ST/OBC priority categories)

## Common rejection reasons
- Name not found in SECC 2011 permanent waitlist (primary blocking reason)
- House already pucca as per satellite survey (PMAY-G uses satellite imagery to verify)
- Family member's income above threshold (discovered during verification)
- Duplicate application (same household applied in Gramin and Urban lists)
- Land not in applicant's name (spouse or parent owns it; transfer not done)
- Aadhaar not seeded with bank account leading to failed DBT

## Common silent/ghosted causes (labeled as inference, not confirmed)
- Application pending at Gram Panchayat prioritization stage — probable if Gram Sabha hasn't met to finalize list
- Satellite verification showing existing structure — house may be recorded as semi-pucca even if it isn't
- Awaiting state share funds release — Centre releases only after state releases matching share
- BLC (Beneficiary-Led Construction) instalment stuck because geo-tagged construction photos not uploaded on AwaasSoft
