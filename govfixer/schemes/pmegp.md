# Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP)

## Category
Employment / Entrepreneurship / Self-Employment

## State Coverage
Pan-India (Implemented by KVIC at national level, KVIBs at state level, DICs at district level)

## Benefit
Subsidy of 15–35% of project cost (margin money), up to ₹50 lakh for manufacturing and ₹20 lakh for service sector:
- 15% for urban general category
- 25% for rural general category
- 25% for urban SC/ST/OBC/Minorities/Women/Ex-servicemen/PWD
- 35% for rural SC/ST/OBC/Minorities/Women/Ex-servicemen/PWD
Bank loan covers 60–75% of project cost; beneficiary contributes 5–10%.

## Eligibility criteria (plain language)
- Age: 18 years and above
- For projects above ₹10 lakh (manufacturing) or ₹5 lakh (service): must have passed at least 8th standard
- Existing units or units already availing government subsidy are NOT eligible
- Self-Help Groups (including SC/ST) are eligible if not already availed of government subsidy
- Charitable trusts and co-operative societies registered under relevant Acts are eligible
- Only one person per family is eligible
- Beneficiary must not have defaulted on any loan from a bank/financial institution

## Required documents (ranked by how often applicants miss them)
1. Aadhaar card
2. Educational qualification certificates (critical for projects above ₹10 lakh)
3. Project report (most common failure point — must be detailed, realistic, with market survey)
4. Caste certificate (for SC/ST/OBC/Minority special rate subsidy)
5. Bank statement (last 6 months) — to show no existing loan default
6. Residence proof (voter ID, ration card, utility bill)
7. EDP (Entrepreneurship Development Programme) training certificate — required BEFORE disbursement in most states

## Common rejection reasons
- Weak/unrealistic project report — most common rejection by bank committee
- Applicant already runs an existing unit or has availed prior government subsidy
- CIBIL score below bank threshold (existing loan default, even small amounts)
- Proposed activity is in the negative list (tobacco, alcohol, meat processing, polythene bags, etc.)
- Age proof shows below 18
- EDP training not completed before loan processing (common process failure)
- Same family member already received PMEGP (only one per family)

## Common silent/ghosted causes (labeled as inference, not confirmed)
- Application stuck at bank's loan committee for review — probable if >45 days post task force meeting
- KVIC/KVIB portal technical issue during submission — application may appear submitted but not registered
- DIC officer's approval pending — some districts have backlogs
- Bank branch not familiar with PMEGP scheme — branch-level ignorance causes indefinite hold

## How to check status
- kviconline.gov.in → PMEGP e-Portal → Application Status
- Helpline: 1800-3000-0034 (KVIC toll-free)
