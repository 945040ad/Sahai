# Scheme: National Social Assistance Programme – Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension (IGNOAPS)

## Category
Social Security / Old Age Pension

## State Coverage
Pan-India (Central scheme, implemented through States; states add top-up)

## Benefit
- Age 60–79: ₹200/month (Central contribution; states add ₹100–₹500 on top)
- Age 80+: ₹500/month (Central contribution; states often add more)
- Bihar state top-up: ₹200–₹400 additional (varies by age band)
- Total (Bihar example): ₹400–₹900/month depending on age

## Eligibility criteria (plain language)
- Age: 60 years or above
- Must belong to a Below Poverty Line (BPL) household (as per state BPL list; SECC 2011 is the basis for many states)
- Must have no or very little regular financial support from family or other sources
- Must be a resident of the state from which applying
- Cannot be drawing pension from any other government source (some states are flexible on this)

## Required documents (ranked by how often applicants miss them)
1. Age proof (Aadhaar card preferred; school certificate, voter ID, birth certificate as alternatives — inconsistency between documents is a top rejection cause)
2. BPL/Ration card (proving Below Poverty Line status)
3. Aadhaar card (for DBT to bank account)
4. Bank account linked to Aadhaar
5. Income certificate from Block Development Officer / Tehsildar
6. Residential/domicile certificate (same district as application)
7. Passport-size photograph

## Common rejection reasons
- Age not verifiable (different ages on different documents — very common for elderly citizens without birth records)
- Not on BPL list (household not enumerated or removed after last BPL survey)
- Already receiving pension from another scheme (e.g., EPFO, state government employee pension)
- Income certificate shows family income above threshold
- Aadhaar not linked to bank account for DBT
- Application submitted in wrong district (must apply in district of current residence)

## Common silent/ghosted causes (labeled as inference)
- Application pending at Block/District Social Welfare Office for verification — common bottleneck
- BPL list not updated at state level despite household being genuinely poor
- Fund not released by state due to budget allocation delays — pension stops without notification

## How to check status
- nsap.nic.in → Beneficiary Status
- Contact Block Development Office or District Social Welfare Office directly
- Bihar specific: serviceonline.bihar.gov.in
