# Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana (PMJJBY)

## Category
Insurance / Social Security / Life Cover

## State Coverage
Pan-India (through banks and post offices)

## Benefit
Life insurance cover of ₹2 lakh on death due to any reason. Annual premium: ₹436/year (auto-debited from bank account in May/June).

## Eligibility criteria (plain language)
- Age: 18 to 50 years (entry age); cover continues till age 55
- Must have a savings bank account (any bank or post office)
- Must give consent for auto-debit of premium from bank account
- Can join any time of year (cover starts from date of enrollment)
- People with multiple bank accounts can enroll under only ONE account

## Required documents (ranked by how often applicants miss them)
1. Bank account (savings) — the only real requirement; Aadhaar linkage recommended for smooth claim
2. Consent-cum-Declaration form (auto-debit authorization) — must be signed and submitted to bank
3. Nominee details — must be clearly registered; missing nominee is a major claim-rejection cause

## Common rejection reasons (mostly at CLAIM stage)
- Nominee not registered or registered nominee details don't match documents at time of claim
- Policy lapsed due to insufficient balance in account when auto-debit attempted (bank tries once; if fails, policy lapses)
- Death within 45 days of enrollment (for non-accidental deaths) — waiting period clause
- Policy already lapsed and renewal not done (annual renewal is automatic only if auto-debit succeeds)
- Multiple policies from same person trying to claim twice (only one policy pays)
- Medical records missing for death claim processing

## Common silent/ghosted causes (at enrollment stage, labeled as inference)
- Auto-debit consent not properly recorded by bank branch — enrollment form submitted but not processed
- Bank did not link enrollment to insurance company (PMJJBY runs through LIC and other insurers via banks)

## How to check status
- Contact your bank branch directly (enrollment is via bank, not a central portal)
- Claim via same bank where policy was taken; bank forwards to insurance company
